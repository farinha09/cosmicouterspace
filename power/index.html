A hommage to the commercial actionhero frenchise photography of the 90s. These photos would be done by anonymous photographers working for advertisment companies and film productions.
The TV series „Mighty Morphin Power Rangers“ is based on the japanese „Super Sentai“. The story follows teenagers turn into superheros that defend the earth against evil aliens. The production had many problems. To save money, the action scences were taken from the japanese version. Many actors reported long working days and low payment. Actors and production were replaced multiple times which led to conflicts between cast and production.
